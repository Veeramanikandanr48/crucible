- A scene of many parts with a few thousand triangles each, such as Lone Monk, now gets levels of detail by default: `addLodsToDenseParts` starts at 4000 triangles instead of 20000, which drew 1.06 instead of 1.80 million triangles and took a courtyard view from 4.36 to 3.38 ms on the GPU (a slight change in far detail; #2144). `bench/glaze-passes` can write a camera move and measure the shimmer and level pops of it (#2157).
- A scene file now picks levels of detail at `lodPixelError` 0.5 instead of 1 (about 21 percent more triangles than at 1 on Lone Monk, less shimmer in a camera move); `"renderer": { "lodPixelError": 1 }` restores the old choice (#2157).
